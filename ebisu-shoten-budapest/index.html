The Story

Named for the god who never looks back

Ebisu is the only one of Japan's seven lucky gods said to be purely native — a fisherman's god, always smiling, always a little late because he can't hear the other gods calling. He is patient. He waits for the good thing to come to him.

Ebisu Shoten — "Ebisu's shop" — opened on a quiet street off Budapest's inner ring with the same idea: slow down, sit at the counter, let the coals do the work. Skewers grilled over binchōtan charcoal, small plates built around what came in that morning, and a back bar stocked deep with sake, shōchū and Japanese whisky.
